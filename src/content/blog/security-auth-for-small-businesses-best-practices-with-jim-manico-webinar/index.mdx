### What You’ll Learn

In this webinar, Jim Manico, the founder of Manicode Security and a well-respected authority in secure coding and security engineering, will share his insights and best practices on security and authentication specifically tailored for small businesses. Here’s what you can expect to learn:

- **Security for SMBs vs. Enterprise**: Understanding the unique challenges and solutions for small to medium-sized businesses compared to larger enterprises.
- **Importance of Early Auth & User Management**: Why having authentication and user management figured out from the beginning can simplify scaling your business.
- **SSO Tax Insights**: Jim’s thoughts on the Single Sign-On (SSO) tax and how it impacts your business.
- **Essential Security Checklist**: A practical checklist of bare minimum security measures every small business should implement.

### About Jim Manico

Jim Manico is the founder of Manicode Security, where he trains software developers on secure coding and security engineering. He is also the co-founder of the LocoMoco Security Conference and an investor/advisor for several security-focused companies. Jim is a frequent speaker on secure software practices and is a member of the JavaOne Rockstar speaker community. He is the author of "Iron-Clad Java: Building Secure Web Applications" from McGraw-Hill.
